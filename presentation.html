Research question

How do middle managers experience sociotechnical misalignment during generative AI adoption?

M. Baumann · GenAI adoption at the middle management
Unpacking the research question
Phenomenon

GenAI value stalls where strategic ambition meets operational reality: in the middle.

Floyd & Wooldridge (1992); Huy (2001)

Theory

Sociotechnical systems: adoption problems are gaps between people, structure, task and technology.

Bostrom & Heinen (1977); Lyytinen & Newman (2008)

Method

Semi-structured interviews with middle managers, analysed inductively.

Gioia et al. (2013)

Research gap

GenAI adoption research looks above and below the middle, but rarely at middle managers.

e.g. Prasad Agrawal (2023); Hammerschmidt et al. (2024); Noy & Zhang (2023). First steps: Jean-Baptiste (2025)

M. Baumann · GenAI adoption at the middle management
Problematization
Prevailing assumption

Middle managers simply implement the GenAI decisions made above them.

This study

Middle managers are where misalignment surfaces and where it is resolved.

Why it mattersThis helps explain why GenAI so often fails to move beyond the pilot stage.
